Smartphone-mountable 3.7-inch E Ink electronic reader XTEINK X3 gets 20% discount on Amazon Japan

Amazon.co.jp is currently holding a time sale offering a 20 percent discount on the XTEINK X3 electronic reader, a compact 3.7-inch E Ink display device that weighs just 58 grams and can be attached to the back of a smartphone using a magnetic mount.

Novelty E Ink device designed for smartphone rear attachment

A compact electronic reader equipped with a 3.7-inch E Ink electronic paper display has appeared on Amazon.co.jp with a special promotional price. The device, known as the XTEINK X3 electronic book reader, features a card-size form factor and weighs a remarkably lightweight 58 grams, making it exceptionally portable for daily commuting and mobile reading.

One of the standout characteristics of the XTEINK X3 is its versatile mounting capability. Users can attach the reader directly to the back of a smartphone using a built-in magnetic attachment system. This design allows individuals to carry both their primary smartphone and a dedicated reading surface together as a unified unit without adding excessive bulk or weight to their pockets.

Practical operational features and expandable storage

Despite its diminutive physical footprint, the electronic reader does not skimp on functionality. The manufacturer engineered the device to support comfortable one-handed operation, ensuring that users can easily turn pages and navigate menus while standing on crowded trains or walking outdoors.

In addition to its convenient handling, the XTEINK X3 provides practical flexibility through expandable storage options. The hardware includes support for SD memory card expansion, allowing readers to store large libraries of books, documents, and reference materials directly on physical media without worrying about internal capacity limits.

Special promotional pricing details on Amazon.co.jp

The portable reading gadget has caught the attention of mobile users due to a current limited-time sale event on Amazon.co.jp. The e-reader is receiving a notable 20 percent discount off its regular retail price.

With the promotional reduction applied, interested buyers can acquire the XTEINK X3 electronic book reader for 11,119 yen. This pricing strategy makes the specialized E Ink secondary display an accessible option for smartphone users looking to add an eye-friendly reading surface to their mobile gear ecosystem.

Por que isso importa

The article is relevant because it highlights a specialized mobile accessory that bridges the gap between dedicated E Ink reading devices and modern smartphones, offering an alternative form factor for users seeking eye-friendly displays on the go.

O que pode acontecer no futuro

If this trend of compact, smartphone-mountable secondary screens continues, it is possible that we may see increased integration between mobile operating systems and auxiliary E Ink panels, and manufacturers might introduce more modular hardware solutions for portable productivity.
