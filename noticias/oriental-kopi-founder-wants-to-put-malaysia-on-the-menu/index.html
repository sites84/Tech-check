Oriental Kopi founder wants to put Malaysia on the menu

Calvin Chan, founder of the Malaysian coffee chain Oriental Kopi, is targeting international markets such as Indonesia and Japan after noticing a lack of Malaysian cuisine during his business travels abroad.

Expansion Plans Abroad

Calvin Chan, the visionary founder behind the popular Malaysia-based food and beverage company Oriental Kopi, is setting his sights on a grand mission to introduce authentic Malaysian culinary traditions to the global stage. During his past business trips centered around his mobile phone enterprise across major regions like China, Taiwan, and Hong Kong, Chan frequently noticed a recurring absence. While it was remarkably simple to locate popular culinary offerings from neighboring nations such as Japan, Thailand, and Vietnam, finding distinct dishes and flavors originating from his home country of Malaysia proved to be a significant challenge.

Halal Certification and Target Markets

To bridge this noticeable global gap, the Malaysia-based enterprise is actively preparing for an ambitious regional and international push. The brand, recognized widely for its signature offerings and distinct café culture, is eyeing crucial markets across Asia. Among the primary targets for upcoming expansion are Indonesia and Japan, alongside other potential overseas territories. A pivotal component of this broader international strategy involves securing and leveraging halal certification to ensure broader consumer reach and compliance in diverse multicultural markets, strengthening the brand's appeal to a wider demographic of coffee and food enthusiasts.

Growing Regional Competition

Oriental Kopi's international ambitions arrive amidst a broader wave of Southeast Asian food and beverage startups scaling up their cross-border operations. Across the region, competitors are rapidly accelerating their footprints. For instance, fellow Malaysian brand ZUS Coffee has announced substantial targets to dramatically expand its retail presence in Thailand. Meanwhile, Indonesian coffee chains are similarly brewing up stronger regional and international ambitions, reflecting a competitive and fast-evolving landscape for Southeast Asian beverage companies seeking a larger slice of the global consumer market.

Por que isso importa

The expansion efforts of regional coffee chains like Oriental Kopi highlight how Southeast Asian food and beverage brands are increasingly seeking international growth, which can reshape culinary tourism and cross-border dining markets.

O que pode acontecer no futuro

If this trend of regional scaling continues, it is possible that Malaysian cuisine may gain significantly higher visibility in major global markets like Japan and Indonesia, potentially inspiring more Southeast Asian food brands to pursue aggressive internationalization strategies.
